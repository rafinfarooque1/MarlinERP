========================================

Gross Profit must be calculated automatically.

Formula:

Gross Profit =
Net Sales
− Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Opening Stock
+ Net Purchases
+ Direct Expenses
− Closing Stock

Net Profit =
Gross Profit
+ Other Income
− Indirect Expenses
