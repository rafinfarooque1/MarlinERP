Current Problem

Salary expense is being generated incorrectly for employees who are resigned or have no attendance.

Implement the following complete payroll accounting workflow.

====================================================
1. Attendance-Based Payroll
====================================================

Salary must ONLY be calculated based on attendance.

Rules:

Present = Full Day Salary

Half Day = Half Salary

Absent = No Salary

Leave With Pay = Salary according to leave policy

Leave Without Pay = No Salary

No attendance = No Salary

Never assume an employee is present.
