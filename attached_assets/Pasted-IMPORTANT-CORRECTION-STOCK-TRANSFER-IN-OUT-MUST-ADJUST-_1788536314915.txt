IMPORTANT CORRECTION — STOCK TRANSFER IN/OUT MUST ADJUST OPENING STOCK, NOT CLOSING STOCK

Fix the Stock Transfer accounting/inventory calculation according to this exact rule:

1. STOCK TRANSFER OUT — SOURCE WAREHOUSE

When stock is transferred OUT from Warehouse A to Warehouse B:

Warehouse A:
    Opening Stock
    MINUS Transfer Out
    = Adjusted Opening Stock

The Transfer Out amount/quantity/value MUST be deducted from OPENING STOCK.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT deduct Transfer Out directly from Closing Stock.
- Do NOT create a separate Closing Stock deduction for Transfer Out.
- Transfer Out must not reduce Closing Stock a second time.
- Avoid any double deduction.

Example:

Source Warehouse:
Opening Stock = ₹100,000
Transfer Out = ₹20,000

Adjusted Opening Stock = ₹80,000

The ₹20,000 Transfer Out must NOT then be deducted again from Closing Stock.

--------------------------------------------------

2. STOCK TRANSFER IN — DESTINATION WAREHOUSE

When stock is transferred IN to Warehouse B:

Warehouse B:
    Opening Stock
    PLUS Transfer In
    = Adjusted Opening Stock

Example:

Destination Warehouse:
Opening Stock = ₹50,000
Transfer In = ₹20,000

Adjusted Opening Stock = ₹70,000

The Transfer In must NOT be added separately to Closing Stock again.

--------------------------------------------------

3. CRITICAL RULE

Stock Transfer In/Out is an OPENING STOCK ADJUSTMENT.

It is NOT a direct Closing Stock adjustment.

Correct logic:

SOURCE:
Opening Stock - Transfer Out = Adjusted Opening Stock

DESTINATION:
Opening Stock + Transfer In = Adjusted Opening Stock

Do NOT implement:

Opening Stock - Transfer Out - Transfer Out again

or

Opening Stock + Transfer In + Transfer In again.

There must be NO double counting.

--------------------------------------------------

4. MONTH-WISE STOCK

Respect the existing monthly stock architecture.

For each month:

Adjusted Opening Stock
+
Purchases
-
Sales/Consumption
=
Closing Stock

Where the opening stock has already incorporated the applicable Transfer In/Transfer Out adjustment.

At the next month's opening:
Previous month's Closing Stock becomes the next month's normal Opening Stock according to the existing system.

Do NOT repeatedly apply the same historical transfer every month.

A transfer must be applied exactly once at the correct opening-stock point.

--------------------------------------------------

5. EXAMPLE

Warehouse A:
Opening Stock = ₹100,000
Transfer Out = ₹20,000
Purchases = ₹30,000
Sales/Consumption = ₹40,000

Adjusted Opening Stock:
₹100,000 - ₹20,000 = ₹80,000

Closing Stock:
₹80,000 + ₹30,000 - ₹40,000
= ₹70,000

Warehouse B:
Opening Stock = ₹50,000
Transfer In = ₹20,000
Purchases = ₹10,000
Sales/Consumption = ₹30,000

Adjusted Opening Stock:
₹50,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹70,000

Closing Stock:
₹70,000 + ₹10,000 - ₹30,000
= ₹50,000

Do NOT add the ₹20,000 Transfer In again to Closing Stock.

--------------------------------------------------

6. COMPANY-WIDE STOCK

Internal transfer must NOT change total company stock value.

Example:

Before transfer:
Warehouse A = ₹100,000
Warehouse B = ₹50,000
Company total = ₹150,000

Transfer ₹20,000 from A to B:

Warehouse A = ₹80,000
Warehouse B = ₹70,000
Company total = ₹150,000

Therefore:

Transfer Out = reduction in source warehouse opening stock
Transfer In = increase in destination warehouse opening stock
Company total = unchanged
