4. LEDGER IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH
==================================================

Inspect the current ledger architecture carefully.

The requirement is:

EVERY financial transaction must reach the corresponding ledger.

Do not allow a situation where:

- Sales report is correct but customer ledger is missing entries
- Receipt report is correct but customer ledger does not show the receipt
- Purchase report is correct but vendor ledger is missing entries
- Payment report is correct but vendor ledger does not show the payment
- Expense appears in expense report but not in the ledger
- Payroll appears in payroll but not in expense/payable
- Cash balance differs from Cash Book
- Bank balance differs from Bank Book
- Customer list differs from customer ledger
- Vendor list differs from vendor ledger
- Chart of Accounts differs from ledger statement
- Dashboard differs from accounting reports

Every accounting transaction must flow through the same authoritative posting mechanism.

Identify any places where the ERP calculates balances independently instead of using the authoritative ledger/accounting data.
