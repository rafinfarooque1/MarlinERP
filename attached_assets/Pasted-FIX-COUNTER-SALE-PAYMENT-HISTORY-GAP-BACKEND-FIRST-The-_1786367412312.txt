FIX: COUNTER-SALE PAYMENT HISTORY GAP — BACKEND-FIRST

The latest full ERP audit found one real operational gap:

When a sale is paid immediately at the counter using Cash, UPI, Bank, or another immediate payment method, the accounting is correct and the money is correctly reflected in the books, BUT the system does not always create the corresponding "Payment Received" history record.

Result:
- Sale accounting is correct.
- Cash/Bank balance is correct.
- Customer balance is correct.
- But the payment-history section of the invoice can be blank.
- Bank reconciliation cannot reliably match counter UPI/Bank collections.
- Approximately 45 existing production bills are affected.

IMPORTANT:
DO NOT change the existing accounting amounts or create duplicate accounting entries.

The objective is ONLY to ensure that every actual counter payment has one corresponding payment-history record.

==================================================
1. INVESTIGATE BEFORE CHANGING
==================================================

Trace the complete backend flow for an immediate-payment sale:

POS / Sales Entry
→ Sale creation
→ Payment mode
→ Payment amount
→ Accounting posting
→ Cash/Bank posting
→ Customer ledger
→ Payment history

Identify exactly why the accounting payment is being posted but the payment-history record is missing.

Find the existing payment-history table/model/service/API.

Determine whether the normal Receipt Voucher/payment flow already has a reusable payment-history creation service.

Reuse the existing service where possible.

Do NOT create a second independent payment system.

==================================================
2. CORRECT BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

For an immediate-payment sale:

Example:

Sale = ₹10,000
Payment Mode = Cash
Amount Paid = ₹10,000

The system must create:

SALE
₹10,000

PAYMENT HISTORY
₹10,000 Cash

CASH LEDGER
₹10,000

CUSTOMER BALANCE
₹0

All records must reference the same sale/payment transaction.

For UPI:

Sale = ₹10,000
Payment Mode = UPI
Amount Paid = ₹10,000

Create exactly one payment-history record linked to that sale and the correct UPI/Bank account.

For Bank:

Sale = ₹10,000
Payment Mode = Bank
Amount Paid = ₹10,000

Create exactly one payment-history record linked to the sale and correct bank account.

==================================================
3. CREDIT SALES
==================================================

If Payment Mode = Credit / Pay Later:

DO NOT create a payment-history record unless actual money was received.

The customer should remain outstanding.

Example:

Sale = ₹10,000
Payment Mode = Credit
Amount Received = ₹0

Result:

Customer Receivable = ₹10,000
Payment History = no payment

==================================================
4. PARTIAL PAYMENT
==================================================

If partial payment is supported:

Sale = ₹10,000
Actual payment = ₹6,000

Then:

Payment History = ₹6,000
Cash/Bank = ₹6,000
Customer outstanding = ₹4,000

Do not create a ₹10,000 payment-history record.

==================================================
5. CUSTOMER ADVANCE
==================================================

Do not confuse payment history with customer advance.

If the customer pays more than the invoice:

Invoice = ₹8,000
Actual payment = ₹8,500

Payment History = ₹8,500

₹8,000 settles the invoice.

₹500 remains as customer credit/advance according to the ERP's existing accounting rules.

Do NOT create duplicate receipt entries.

==================================================
6. EDIT SALE
==================================================

When editing an already-paid sale:

Compare the OLD payment with the NEW payment.

Example:

Old sale:
₹10,000 paid by Cash

New sale:
₹9,000 paid by Cash

The backend must correctly update/reverse the related payment accounting and payment-history record.

Do NOT create another ₹9,000 payment on top of the existing ₹10,000.

The final accounting and payment history must represent only the final valid transaction.

If changing payment mode:

Cash → UPI

the payment-history record and accounting must correctly reflect the new payment account.

==================================================
7. DELETE / CANCEL / REVERSE
==================================================

If a paid sale is deleted, cancelled, or reversed according to the ERP's existing rules:

The related payment-history record must also be handled correctly.

No orphan payment-history record may remain.

No orphan cash/bank entry may remain.

No duplicate reversal may be created.

Do not bypass the existing accounting reversal mechanism.

==================================================
8. LOCATION
==================================================

Payment history must inherit the accounting location of the sale.

Example:

Selling Location:
Frozen Hub - Ragiguda

Then:

Sale → Ragiguda
Payment History → Ragiguda
Cash/Bank → Ragiguda
Customer Ledger → Ragiguda
Dashboard → Ragiguda
Reports → Ragiguda

Never accidentally post the payment history or accounting to Head Office.

The creator/user must not override the selected accounting location.

==================================================
10. DUPLICATE PROTECTION
==================================================

This is critical.

The backend must be idempotent.

If the same sale/payment operation is processed twice because of:

- double click
- page refresh
- API retry
- network retry
- background retry

the system must NOT create two payment-history records or two accounting payments.

Use the existing transaction/reference identifiers to prevent duplicates.

There must be exactly one payment-history record for one actual payment.

==================================================
11. DO NOT ALTER ACCOUNTING TOTALS
==================================================

The audit already confirmed that:

Trial Balance balances.
Balance Sheet balances.
Cash/Bank balances reconcile.
Customer balances reconcile.
Vendor balances reconcile.

Therefore:

DO NOT change the accounting calculation merely to fix payment history.

The payment-history record must be attached to the existing valid payment/accounting transaction.

Do not create a second debit/credit just to populate the payment-history screen.
