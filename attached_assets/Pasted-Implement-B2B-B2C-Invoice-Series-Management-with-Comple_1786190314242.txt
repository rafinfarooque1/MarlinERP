Implement B2B/B2C Invoice Series Management with Complete Monthly Locking.

BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The ERP must maintain separate B2B and B2C invoice serial series.

If a customer does not have GSTIN:
→ Sales invoice = B2C

If a customer has a valid GSTIN:
→ Sales invoice = B2B

If GSTIN is added while the accounting month is OPEN:
→ Existing eligible B2C invoices for that customer in that OPEN month must automatically convert to B2B.
→ B2B serial numbers must be assigned.
→ B2C serial numbers must automatically be resequenced in the background.

Once a month is LOCKED:
→ NOTHING in that month can be changed automatically or manually unless an Admin explicitly unlocks the month.

==================================================
1. SEPARATE B2B AND B2C SERIES
==================================================

Maintain separate invoice series:

B2B/2026-27/0001
B2B/2026-27/0002
B2B/2026-27/0003

B2C/2026-27/0001
B2C/2026-27/0002
B2C/2026-27/0003

Do NOT mention warehouse/location in the visible invoice serial number.

The existing location-wise numbering architecture must remain intact internally.

==================================================
2. CUSTOMER GSTIN RULE
==================================================

Customer with valid GSTIN:

→ New Sales invoice = B2B

Customer without GSTIN:

→ New Sales invoice = B2C

Use the actual Customer Master GSTIN.

Do not determine B2B/B2C from:

- Customer name
- Previous invoice
- User selection
- Browser
- Location
- Hardcoded values

==================================================
3. B2C → B2B CONVERSION WHILE MONTH IS OPEN
==================================================

Example:

Customer initially has no GSTIN.

The customer has already generated:

B2C/2026-27/0001
B2C/2026-27/0002
B2C/2026-27/0003
...
B2C/2026-27/0010

Later, while the month is OPEN, the customer GSTIN is added.

The system must automatically:

1. Detect the GSTIN update.
2. Find all eligible B2C sales invoices belonging to that customer in the OPEN month.
3. Convert them to B2B.
4. Assign valid B2B serial numbers.
5. Remove those invoice numbers from the B2C sequence.
6. Resequence the remaining OPEN-month B2C invoices automatically in the background.
7. Update GST classification and reports.
8. Keep the original accounting transaction intact.

==================================================
4. MONTH LOCKING
==================================================

Add a proper Month Locking system.

Every accounting month must have a status:

OPEN

or

LOCKED

Example:

April 2026 → LOCKED
May 2026 → LOCKED
June 2026 → OPEN
July 2026 → OPEN

Only Admin can lock or unlock a month.

==================================================
5. WHAT MONTH LOCKING MEANS
==================================================

When a month is LOCKED:

ABSOLUTELY NOTHING belonging to that month can be changed.

This includes:

Sales
POS Sales
Quotations converted to Sales
Purchase Bills
Purchase Returns
Sales Returns
Receipt Vouchers
Payment Vouchers
Journal Vouchers
Expenses
Production
Stock Transfers
Inventory Adjustments
Cash Entries
Bank Entries
Payroll
GST-related transaction data
Customer transactions
Vendor transactions
Ledger entries
Invoice classifications
B2B/B2C status
Invoice serial numbers
Invoice dates
Purchase dates
Sales dates
Payment dates
Receipt dates
MFG/Expiry data where the change affects a locked transaction
Opening balances belonging to that period
Imported transactions

No edit.

No delete.

No automatic update.

No automatic resequencing.

No automatic reclassification.

No background process may modify locked-month data.

==================================================
6. LOCKED MONTH AND CUSTOMER GSTIN
==================================================

Example:

June is LOCKED.

Customer had:

B2C/2026-27/0010

Customer GSTIN is added in July.

The June invoice MUST remain:

B2C/2026-27/0010

It must NOT automatically become B2B.

It must NOT receive a new B2B serial.

It must NOT cause B2C June invoices to be renumbered.

The locked month's history is frozen.

==================================================
7. UNLOCK MONTH
==================================================

Only Admin can unlock a month.

When Admin unlocks a month:

Status:

LOCKED → OPEN

Only then may permitted corrections occur.

Record every unlock in the Audit Log:

- Month
- Unlocked By
- Date
- Time
- Reason

Require Admin confirmation before unlocking.

==================================================
8. LOCK MONTH
==================================================

Only Admin can lock a month.

Before locking, show:

"This will permanently lock all transactions, invoice numbers, accounting entries and reports for this month. No changes or automatic reclassification will be allowed until an Administrator unlocks the month."

Require confirmation.

After locking:

- Freeze the month.
- Stop all background modifications.
- Stop B2B/B2C automatic conversion.
- Stop serial resequencing.
- Stop editing.
- Stop deletion.

Record:

- Month
- Locked By
- Date
- Time

==================================================
9. FRONTEND VALIDATION
==================================================

If user attempts to edit a locked transaction:

Show:

" This month is locked. Transactions in a locked month cannot be modified. Contact an Administrator to unlock the month."

Do not open the save operation.

If user attempts to delete:

Show the same locked-month warning.

==================================================
10. BACKEND VALIDATION
==================================================

IMPORTANT:

Locking MUST NOT be implemented only in the frontend.

Every backend create/update/delete endpoint must verify the accounting period.

Before any transaction is modified:

1. Identify transaction date.
2. Identify accounting month.
3. Check month status.
4. If LOCKED:
   Reject the operation.

This protection must apply even if someone calls the API directly.

==================================================
11. BACKGROUND JOB PROTECTION
==================================================

Every automated process must check month status before modifying records.

Especially:

- B2B/B2C conversion
- Invoice resequencing
- GST updates
- Payment allocation
- Outstanding recalculation
- Payroll
- Inventory recalculation
- Import processing

If transaction belongs to a LOCKED month:

DO NOT modify it.

==================================================
12. ACCOUNTING INTEGRITY
==================================================

Changing B2C → B2B must NOT create:

- Duplicate sales
- Duplicate journals
- Duplicate receipts
- Duplicate payments
- Duplicate stock movements

Only classification and invoice-series information may change while the month is OPEN.

Accounting values must remain identical unless a genuine accounting correction is explicitly performed.

==================================================
13. SERIAL NUMBER RESEQUENCING
==================================================

For OPEN months:

If invoices move from B2C to B2B:

Automatically remove unnecessary gaps in the OPEN B2C sequence.

Example:

Before:

B2C/001
B2C/002
B2C/003
B2C/004
B2C/005

B2C/003 moves to B2B.

After:

B2C/001
B2C/002
B2C/003
B2C/004

The remaining OPEN invoices may be resequenced.

For LOCKED months:

NO resequencing under any circumstance.

==================================================
17. MONTH OPENING
==================================================

At the beginning of a new month:

Create a new OPEN accounting period.

Start the appropriate B2B/B2C running series according to the ERP's existing numbering rules.

Previous locked months must remain untouched.

==================================================
18. REGRESSION TESTING
==================================================

Test:

1. Customer without GSTIN creates B2C invoice.
2. Add GSTIN while month is OPEN.
3. Existing eligible B2C invoices convert to B2B.
4. B2B serials are assigned correctly.
5. B2C series is resequenced.
6. Accounting entries remain unchanged.
7. Lock the month.
8. Add GSTIN to a customer.
9. Verify locked-month invoices do NOT change.
10. Attempt to edit locked invoice.
11. Verify backend rejects it.
12. Attempt to delete locked invoice.
13. Verify backend rejects it.
14. Attempt automatic B2C → B2B conversion.
15. Verify background process skips locked month.
16. Admin unlocks the month.
17. Verify controlled corrections can happen again.
18. Lock the month again.
19. Verify it becomes completely immutable.

==================================================
FINAL BUSINESS RULE
==================================================

OPEN MONTH:

Transactions can be corrected according to permissions.

B2C → B2B automatic conversion is allowed.

B2C serial resequencing is allowed.

LOCKED MONTH:

The month is completely frozen.

Nothing can be changed.

Nothing can be deleted.

Nothing can be automatically reclassified.

No serial number can change.

No background process can modify it.

Only Admin can unlock the month.

After Admin unlocks it, it becomes OPEN again and controlled corrections are permitted.

The month-lock restriction must be enforced in BOTH frontend and backend.

The accounting database must never allow a locked month's historical records to be silently changed.
