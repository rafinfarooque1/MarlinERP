TASK: CHANGE POS DISCOUNT SYSTEM — PER-UNIT ITEM DISCOUNT + BILL-LEVEL DISCOUNT

Implement this in the Point of Sale create AND edit-sale workflows.

IMPORTANT:
There must be TWO independent discount concepts:

1. ITEM DISCOUNT — discount per unit / on MRP
2. BILL DISCOUNT — one discount on the whole invoice

Do not mix these two calculations.

==================================================
1. CHANGE CURRENT ITEM DISCOUNT BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

Currently the Discount ₹ field appears to deduct the entered amount only once
from the total line amount.

Example of CURRENT behaviour:

MRP = ₹100
Qty = 10
Discount = ₹10

Current system effectively calculates:

₹100 × 10 = ₹1,000
₹1,000 - ₹10 = ₹990

THIS MUST CHANGE.

The Discount ₹ field beside each item must mean:

"DISCOUNT PER UNIT"

or simply display:

Discount / Unit (₹)

Example:

MRP = ₹100
Discount / Unit = ₹10

Effective selling price per unit = ₹90.

Qty = 10

Line value before applicable GST treatment =:

₹90 × 10 = ₹900

Therefore the ₹10 discount applies to EVERY UNIT.

==================================================
2. ITEM DISCOUNT FORMULA
==================================================

For every line:

effectiveUnitPrice = MRP - unitDiscount

Then calculate the line using:

effectiveUnitPrice × quantity

Example:

MRP                  ₹100
Discount / Unit       ₹10
Effective Price       ₹90
Qty                     10
Effective Line Value ₹900

Do NOT calculate:

(MRP × Qty) - Discount

Instead calculate:

(MRP - DiscountPerUnit) × Qty

==================================================
3. VALIDATION
==================================================

Item discount cannot be:

negative
greater than MRP

Therefore:

0 <= unitDiscount <= MRP

Example:

MRP ₹100
Discount ₹100 = allowed
Discount ₹101 = rejected

Backend must enforce this too.

==================================================
4. TAXABLE CHECKBOX MUST CONTINUE WORKING
==================================================

The POS already has the new:

☑ Taxable (+GST on top)

checkbox.

The item discount must be applied BEFORE the GST calculation.

The effective price after item discount becomes the price used for determining
the taxable value.

--------------------------------------------------
A. TAXABLE CHECKED
--------------------------------------------------

Taxable checked means GST EXCLUSIVE.

Example:

MRP = ₹200
Item Discount / Unit = ₹20

Effective unit price = ₹180

Qty = 1
GST = 5%

Taxable value = ₹180
GST = ₹9
Grand line amount = ₹189

For Qty 10:

Taxable value:
₹180 × 10 = ₹1,800

GST:
₹90

Line total:
₹1,890

--------------------------------------------------
B. TAXABLE NOT CHECKED
--------------------------------------------------

Taxable unchecked means the effective selling price is GST INCLUSIVE.

Example:

MRP = ₹200
Item Discount = ₹20

Effective GST-inclusive price = ₹180

GST 5%

Taxable value must be extracted from ₹180:

₹180 / 1.05 = ₹171.428...

GST = ₹8.571...

Final amount remains:

₹180

For Qty 10:

Final line amount = ₹1,800

Extract taxable value and GST from that ₹1,800 using the existing correct
CGST/SGST/IGST rules.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT calculate GST on ₹200 after applying a ₹20 discount.

The discount reduces the selling price FIRST.

==================================================
5. ADD SECOND DISCOUNT — BILL DISCOUNT
==================================================

Add a completely separate field in:

INVOICE SUMMARY

called:

Bill Discount (₹)

This discount applies ONCE to the entire invoice.

Example:

Items after item-level discounts = ₹5,000
Bill Discount = ₹500

Final payable amount = ₹4,500
subject to the correct GST/tax allocation rules described below.

This must NOT be confused with item discount.

==================================================
6. BILL DISCOUNT TAX TREATMENT
==================================================

Do NOT simply calculate GST first and subtract ₹500 from Grand Total.

The invoice-level discount reduces the consideration for the goods.

Therefore distribute the Bill Discount proportionately across eligible invoice
lines before determining the final taxable values and GST amounts.

Example:

Line A pre-bill-discount basis = ₹3,000
Line B pre-bill-discount basis = ₹2,000

Total = ₹5,000

Bill Discount = ₹500

Allocation:

Line A gets 60% of discount = ₹300
Line B gets 40% = ₹200

Then calculate final taxable value/GST for each line using its adjusted amount
and its own:

GST rate
CGST/SGST/IGST treatment
taxable inclusive/exclusive setting

This is especially important when the invoice contains items with different GST
rates.

Do not use a mathematically incorrect shortcut that changes GST disproportionately.

Handle rounding carefully so allocated discount totals exactly equal the entered
Bill Discount.
