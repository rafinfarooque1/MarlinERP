Refactor the ERP to remove the separate "Customer Advances" accounting structure completely.

Business Decision (Final)

This ERP will NOT maintain a separate "Customer Advances" liability account.

Instead, customer advances will always be represented as a CREDIT (negative) balance in the customer's normal Sundry Debtor ledger.

This is the final accounting design and must be implemented consistently across the entire ERP.

====================================================
1. Remove Customer Advances
====================================================

Remove the entire "Customer Advances" group from the Chart of Accounts.

Remove every automatically created:

Customer Name – Advance

ledger.

Do not create these ledgers for new customers.

====================================================

====================================================

Every customer must have ONLY ONE ledger.

Customer Name

under

Assets
→ Current Assets
→ Sundry Debtors

There should never be a second advance ledger.

====================================================
3. Accounting Entries
====================================================

When customer pays an advance:

Debit
Cash / Bank

Credit
Customer Ledger (Sundry Debtor)

The customer ledger will become a CREDIT (negative) balance.

Example:

Customer pays ₹10,000 advance

Dr Cash ₹10,000

Cr Customer Ledger ₹10,000

Customer Ledger:

Credit Balance ₹10,000

(No separate advance ledger.)
