TASK: Implement Separate Sales Invoice Numbering for B2B and B2C (SB2B / SB2C Series)

The ERP currently uses a single invoice numbering series for all Sales invoices. I need separate invoice numbering for B2B and B2C Sales Bills.

This change applies ONLY to Sales Bills (Point of Sale / Sales Module). Do NOT change the numbering of Purchase, Receipt, Payment, Journal, Contra, Credit Note, Debit Note, Stock Transfer, Production, or any other vouchers.

====================================================
NEW SALES BILL SERIES
====================================================

Implement two independent Sales Bill numbering series:

1. SB2B (Sales Business-to-Business)
2. SB2C (Sales Business-to-Consumer)

Example:

SB2B/2026-27/000001
SB2B/2026-27/000002
SB2B/2026-27/000003

SB2C/2026-27/000001
SB2C/2026-27/000002
SB2C/2026-27/000003

Each series must maintain its own independent running sequence.

====================================================
B2B IDENTIFICATION
====================================================

A Sales Bill is considered B2B when:

• Customer has a valid GST Number.
• Customer is GST registered.
• GST Number is not blank.

Automatically generate:

SB2B/FY/Running Number

Example:

SB2B/2026-27/000001

No manual selection is required.

====================================================
B2C IDENTIFICATION
====================================================

A Sales Bill is considered B2C when:

• Customer GST Number is blank.
• Walk-in Customer.
• Retail Customer.
• Unregistered Customer.

Automatically generate:

SB2C/FY/Running Number

Example:

SB2C/2026-27/000001

====================================================
EXISTING SALES BILLS
====================================================

Update all existing Sales Bills.

Rules:

If Customer has GST Number

Convert invoice number into

SB2B/FY/Running Number

If Customer has NO GST Number

Convert invoice number into

SB2C/FY/Running Number

Maintain the original chronological order based on invoice date and creation order.

Do NOT modify:

• Invoice Date
• Customer
• Stock
• Accounting Entries
• GST Entries
• Ledger Entries
• Payments
• Receivables
• Inventory
• Financial Reports

Only update the invoice numbering.

====================================================
