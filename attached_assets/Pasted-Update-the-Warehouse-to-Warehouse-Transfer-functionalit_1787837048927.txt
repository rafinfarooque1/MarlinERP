Update the **Warehouse-to-Warehouse Transfer** functionality and its reports.

### Required Accounting and GST Treatment

A warehouse-to-warehouse transfer should be treated as a **Sale transaction from the source warehouse** and a corresponding **Purchase transaction for the destination warehouse**, wherever GST and inter-warehouse taxable supply rules are applicable.

#### 1. Source Warehouse — Sale

When stock is transferred:

* The **From Warehouse** must record the transaction as a **Sale/Outward Supply**.
* Stock should be reduced from the source warehouse.
* The transaction must appear in the relevant **Sales Reports**.
* GST, taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST, and total invoice value should be calculated and recorded according to the applicable transaction configuration.
* The accounting and GST reports should treat this as a sale/outward supply.

#### 2. Destination Warehouse — Purchase

For the receiving warehouse:

* The **To Warehouse** must record the transaction as a **Purchase/Inward Supply**.
* Stock should be added to the destination warehouse.
* The transaction should appear in the relevant **Purchase Reports**.
* Applicable GST/input tax details should be recorded appropriately.

### Report Changes

The current Warehouse Transfer report should clearly reflect the transaction as:

* **Sale / Outward Supply** for the From Warehouse
* **Purchase / Inward Supply** for the To Warehouse

The reports should show all relevant details, including:

* Transfer/Invoice Number
* Date
* From Warehouse
* To Warehouse
* Customer/Branch or receiving entity details, as applicable
* Item details
* Quantity
* Rate
* Taxable Value
* GST details
* Total Invoice Value

### PDF / Print Format

The PDF generated for a Warehouse-to-Warehouse Transfer should **not appear as a simple stock transfer document**.

Instead, it should be formatted and presented like a proper **GST Sales Invoice / Sale Bill** for the source warehouse.

The PDF should include the standard sale invoice details, such as:

* **TAX INVOICE / SALES INVOICE**
* Invoice Number
* Invoice Date
* Seller / Source Warehouse details
* Buyer / Destination Warehouse details
* GSTIN details, wherever applicable
* Billing and shipping details, if required
* Item-wise quantity, rate and taxable value
* CGST + SGST or IGST, as applicable
* Total taxable amount
* Total GST amount
* Grand Total
* Amount in words
* Authorized signatory section

### Important

The system should maintain the relationship between both sides of the same transaction. A single Warehouse-to-Warehouse Transfer should create or represent:

**Source Warehouse → Sale / Outward Supply**

**Destination Warehouse → Purchase / Inward Supply**

Both records must remain linked to the same transfer/invoice transaction for reporting, tracking, stock reconciliation, accounting, and GST purposes.

The existing stock movement functionality should continue to work correctly, but the **financial, reporting, GST, and PDF presentation must reflect the transaction as a Sale from the source and a Purchase at the destination**.
