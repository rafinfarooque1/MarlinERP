Update the Voucher Print Layout to A4 Landscape with ONE Voucher Per Half-Page

I need the Payment Voucher, Receipt Voucher, and Journal Voucher printing to work like a physical A4 sheet that can be cut in half.

PAPER FORMAT

- Paper size: A4
- Orientation: Landscape
- A4 dimensions: 297 × 210 mm
- Divide the A4 landscape page vertically into TWO equal A5-size areas.
- Each half should be approximately 148.5 × 210 mm.
- The voucher must be designed to fit completely inside ONE half of the A4 page.

IMPORTANT PRINTING BEHAVIOR

I do NOT want two vouchers automatically printed on one A4 page.

I want:

CASE 1 — PRINT ONE VOUCHER

When the user prints/downloads ONE voucher:

- Generate an A4 Landscape page.
- Place the voucher ONLY on the LEFT HALF of the A4 page.
- Keep the RIGHT HALF completely blank.
- Add a clear vertical cutting/divider line between the two halves if appropriate.
- The voucher must occupy only one half of the A4 page.
- Do NOT duplicate the same voucher on the right side.

After printing:

I should be able to cut the A4 sheet vertically in the middle and get one complete voucher.

CASE 2 — PRINT ANOTHER VOUCHER LATER

If I print another single voucher separately:

- Again generate a fresh A4 Landscape page.
- Place that voucher ONLY on the LEFT HALF.
- Keep the RIGHT HALF blank.

This allows me to:

1. Print Voucher A.
2. Cut the A4 sheet in half.
3. Keep the unused half.
4. Later use that remaining half separately if needed.

DO NOT automatically place two vouchers side-by-side.
