TASK: Unify Customer Receipt Collection with Cash & Bank Accounts

Currently, there are two different payment collection interfaces.

Customer Payment Collection (Sales)
- Shows only:
  • Cash
  • Bank
  • UPI

Vendor Payment
- Shows actual Cash & Bank accounts created in the Cash & Bank module.

The Customer Receipt screen must work exactly like the Vendor Payment screen.

==========================================================
Required Behaviour
==========================================================

Remove the simple payment method dropdown:

Cash
Bank
UPI

Replace it with:

Receive Into (Cash / Bank Account)

The dropdown must list the actual accounts created in the Cash & Bank module.

Example

Cash Accounts
------------
Cash
Frozen Hub - Ragiguda Cash
Frozen Hub - Calicut Cash

Bank Accounts
-------------
HDFC Current
ICICI Current
Axis Bank
UPI Collections
etc.

Exactly the same dropdown used in Vendor Payments.

==========================================================
Warehouse Filtering
==========================================================

The dropdown must only show accounts assigned to the currently selected location.

Example

Current Location

Frozen Hub - Ragiguda

Show

✓ Frozen Hub - Ragiguda Cash
✓ Ragiguda HDFC
✓ Ragiguda ICICI
✓ Ragiguda UPI Collections

Do NOT show

Frozen Hub - Calicut Cash
Calicut HDFC
Head Office Cash

----------------------------------------------------------

If Current Location = Head Office

Show only Head Office accounts.

----------------------------------------------------------

If Current Location = All Locations

Only administrators may choose from all accounts.

==========================================================
Accounting
==========================================================

When payment is collected:

Debit

Selected Cash/Bank Account

Credit

Customer Ledger

Update

Customer Outstanding
Customer Ledger
Cash Book
Bank Book
Cash & Bank Screen
Dashboard
Trial Balance
Balance Sheet

Everything must update against the selected location.

Never post to the generic "Cash" account unless that account was explicitly selected.

==========================================================
Existing Receipts
==========================================================

Viewing old receipts should continue working.

If an old receipt used the legacy method:

Cash

Display the mapped Cash account.

If

Bank

Display the mapped Bank account.

No existing data should break.

==========================================================
UI
==========================================================

Use exactly the same account selector already used in:

Vendor Payment
Payment Voucher
Cash & Bank

Do not create another payment selector.

Use one common component throughout the ERP.

==========================================================
Validation
==========================================================

Only active accounts assigned to the selected warehouse/location should appear.

Inactive accounts must not be selectable.

Deleted accounts must not appear.

==========================================================
Regression Testing
==========================================================

Verify:

✓ Customer Receipt
✓ Vendor Payment
✓ Receipt Voucher
✓ Payment Voucher
✓ POS Payment Collection
✓ Sales Invoice Payment Collection

All should use the same Cash & Bank account selector.

Verify posting updates:

• Customer Ledger
• Cash/Bank Ledger
• Trial Balance
• Balance Sheet
• Dashboard
• Cash Book
• Bank Book

Everything must reconcile to the paisa.

The objective is to remove the old "Cash / Bank / UPI" selector and use the real Cash & Bank accounts everywhere in the ERP so all receipts and payments post into actual accounts assigned to each warehouse.
