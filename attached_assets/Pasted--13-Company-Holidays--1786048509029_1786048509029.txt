==========================================================
13. Company Holidays
==========================================================

The ERP should support Company Holidays.

Admin should be able to define holidays such as:

• Sundays
• Second Saturday
• Fourth Saturday
• National Holidays
• Festival Holidays
• Company Declared Holidays

These holidays should automatically appear in the attendance calendar.

==========================================================
14. Attendance Fix – Holiday Option
==========================================================

In the "Fix Attendance" screen, add two new options:

• Company Holiday
• Weekly Off

Current options become:

Present
Half Day
Casual Leave
Sick Leave
Absent
Company Holiday
Weekly Off

==========================================================
15. Company Holiday Behaviour
==========================================================

If "Company Holiday" is selected:

• Employee receives full salary.
• Attendance is marked as Company Holiday.
• No Casual Leave is deducted.
• No Sick Leave is deducted.
• It should not count as Absent.

==========================================================
16. Weekly Off Behaviour
==========================================================

The company should be able to configure weekly offs.

Examples:

Sunday
Second Saturday
Friday
Any custom weekly off

For each Weekly Off, the company should choose one of two payroll policies.

Option A – Paid Weekly Off

• Employee receives full salary.
• No leave balance is reduced.

Option B – Weekly Off Uses Casual Leave

• Employee receives full salary.
• One Casual Leave is automatically deducted.
• If Casual Leave balance is exhausted:
    - Ask Admin before saving, OR
    - Automatically convert that day to Absent based on company settings.

==========================================================
17. Attendance Correction for Holidays
==========================================================

When fixing attendance for any date that is already marked as:

• Company Holiday
• Weekly Off

Admin should be able to override it if required.

Examples:

Weekly Off → Present
Weekly Off → Casual Leave
Weekly Off → Sick Leave
Weekly Off → Absent
Company Holiday → Present
Company Holiday → Absent

The payroll should automatically recalculate after the correction.

==========================================================
18. Employee Leave Balance
==========================================================

Whenever Casual Leave is consumed through:

• Attendance Fix
• Weekly Off (if company policy uses Casual Leave)

the employee's Casual Leave balance must reduce automatically.

Leave Summary should always display:

Casual Leave
Allocated
Used
Remaining

Sick Leave
Allocated
Used
Remaining

==========================================================
19. Validation
==========================================================

Verify:

✓ Company Holiday pays full salary.
✓ Company Holiday does NOT reduce any leave.
✓ Paid Weekly Off pays full salary.
✓ Weekly Off configured to use Casual Leave reduces one Casual Leave.
✓ If Casual Leave balance is zero, the ERP warns Admin or converts the day to Absent according to company policy.
✓ Attendance correction updates payroll automatically.
✓ Leave balances remain accurate after every correction.
