Implement a major update to the Marlin ERP Sales, Payment Collection, Cash Deposit, Reconciliation, and Accounting Ledger workflow.

IMPORTANT:
Before changing code, inspect the existing architecture, database schema, sales/POS flow, invoice flow, account_ledgers structure, journal/accounting logic, branch/outlet structure, payment fields, PDF/WhatsApp invoice functionality, and existing APIs.

Preserve all existing working functionality.

Do not create duplicate accounting systems or duplicate ledger concepts. Extend the existing architecture cleanly.

All database migrations must be idempotent and safe for existing data.

==================================================
1. CORE BUSINESS FLOW
==================================================

Change the sales/payment lifecycle to:

SALE
→ GENERATE INVOICE
→ COLLECT / RECORD PAYMENT
→ depending on payment method:
   CASH → Branch Cash Ledger
   UPI/Card/Bank Transfer/Other electronic methods → Reconciliation
→ RECONCILE / DEPOSIT
→ Bank Ledger

Generating an invoice and receiving payment MUST be separate actions.

An invoice must not automatically be considered paid merely because it was generated.

Invoice status = PAID
Payment reconciliation status = PENDING

==================================================

Accounting concept:

Dr Branch/Outlet Cash
Cr Sales / Receivable clearing as required by the existing accounting architecture

Do not create duplicate sales revenue if revenue was already recognized when the invoice was posted.

Use the existing double-entry accounting design correctly.

7. ELECTRONIC PAYMENT RECONCILIATION
==================================================

Payments received through:

UPI
Card
Bank Transfer
Other settlement-based electronic methods

must NOT immediately increase the final Bank ledger.

Instead, create reconciliation/payment-clearing entries.

Conceptually:

Customer pays ₹1,000 via UPI.

Payment becomes:

Payment status = RECEIVED
Reconciliation status = PENDING

Accounting should use an appropriate clearing account, for example:

Dr Payment Settlement / Reconciliation Clearing ₹1,000
Cr Receivable / appropriate invoice clearing account ₹1,000

Do NOT debit the final bank ledger yet.

The exact journal entry must respect the existing accounting architecture and avoid double-posting revenue.

15. CASH DEPOSIT ACCOUNTING FLOW
==================================================

When cash is physically deposited:

Create a cash-deposit/reconciliation record.

The amount must no longer be treated as freely available physical outlet cash once the deposit is recorded.

Use a Cash Deposit / Cash-in-Transit clearing mechanism if appropriate to the existing accounting architecture.

Conceptually:

At deposit initiation:

Dr Cash Deposit / Cash-in-Transit Clearing
Cr Branch Cash

Then create a pending reconciliation item.

When bank credit is confirmed/reconciled:

Dr Selected Bank Account
Cr Cash Deposit / Cash-in-Transit Clearing

This prevents double counting cash as both:

cash physically in outlet

and

cash pending deposit.

For cash deposits, bank charges/deductions should also be supported if required:

Gross deposit
- charges/deductions
= net bank credit

All entries must remain balanced.

22. CRITICAL ACCOUNTING RULE
==================================================

Do not duplicate revenue.

Invoice generation, payment collection, reconciliation, and bank settlement are different financial events.

The implementation must understand the existing accounting posting logic before adding journals.

A payment moving through:

Receivable
→ Cash/Clearing
→ Bank

must not create Sales Revenue multiple times.

Every journal entry must satisfy:

TOTAL DEBITS = TOTAL CREDITS
