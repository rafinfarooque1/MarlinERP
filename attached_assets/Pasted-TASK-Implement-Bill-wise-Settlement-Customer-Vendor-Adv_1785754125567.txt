TASK: Implement Bill-wise Settlement, Customer/Vendor Advance Management, and Automatic Outstanding Adjustment

OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the Receipt Voucher and Payment Voucher modules to support standard ERP bill-wise accounting.

Currently, creating a Receipt Voucher or Payment Voucher only posts accounting entries.

It does NOT settle customer or vendor bills.

Implement proper bill-wise adjustment similar to Tally, SAP, Zoho Books and ERPNext.

Receipt Voucher and Payment Voucher should behave exactly like a standard ERP.

Selecting a customer or vendor should display outstanding bills for settlement.

Payments should automatically clear invoices.

Excess receipts/payments should automatically create Customer/Vendor Advances.

Future invoices should automatically offer to consume available advances.

Outstanding balances, ledgers, dashboards and reports should always remain synchronized with complete accounting integrity.
