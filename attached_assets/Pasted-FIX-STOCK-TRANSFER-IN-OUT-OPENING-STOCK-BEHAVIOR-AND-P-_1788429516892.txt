FIX STOCK TRANSFER IN/OUT – OPENING STOCK BEHAVIOR AND P&L EXCLUSION

IMPORTANT:
First inspect the existing stock transfer, opening stock, closing stock, stock ledger, inventory valuation and P&L calculation logic.

Do NOT blindly rewrite existing accounting or historical data.

BUSINESS REQUIREMENT

Our ERP uses a monthly stock calculation.

When stock is transferred from one warehouse to another warehouse:

SOURCE WAREHOUSE:
The transferred quantity must be reduced from the source warehouse's stock/opening stock calculation.

DESTINATION WAREHOUSE:
The transferred quantity must be added to the destination warehouse's stock/opening stock calculation.

MOST IMPORTANT:

Stock Transfer In and Stock Transfer Out are INTERNAL STOCK MOVEMENTS.

They must NOT create income, expense, sales, COGS or profit.

Therefore:

Transfer In → increases receiving warehouse stock

Transfer Out → decreases sending warehouse stock

BUT:

Transfer In/Out → MUST NOT affect P&L.

--------------------------------------------------
MONTH-WISE STOCK LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------

The ERP currently works with monthly Opening Stock and Closing Stock.

For a selected month, calculate stock correctly using the existing ERP design.

Example:

April Opening Stock = 100 KG

During April:

Transfer Out = 20 KG

Then April stock available from that opening quantity becomes:

100 - 20 = 80 KG

If another warehouse receives the 20 KG:

Receiving warehouse stock:

Opening Stock + 20 KG Transfer In

Example:

Receiving warehouse Opening Stock = 50 KG

Transfer In = 20 KG

Stock = 70 KG

The transfer must not be treated as a purchase or income.

OPENING STOCK REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT:

The business requirement is that Transfer In/Transfer Out must be reflected through the Opening Stock / stock calculation mechanism currently used by this ERP.

However, DO NOT duplicate the transfer.

If a transfer is already represented in the opening-stock calculation, do not also add it again through Transfer In/Out.

There must be ONE consistent stock movement.

For every product/location/month:

Opening Stock
+ Transfer In
- Transfer Out
+ Purchases
+ Production
- Sales
± Adjustments
= Closing Stock

Use the ERP's existing stock calculation architecture and ensure Transfer In/Out is incorporated exactly once.

NEXT MONTH:

Next month's Opening Stock must be based on the previous month's final Closing Stock according to the ERP's monthly architecture.

Do NOT repeatedly carry the same historical transfer into every future month.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------

Warehouse A:

Opening Stock = 1,000 KG

Transfer Out = 200 KG

Warehouse B:

Opening Stock = 500 KG

Transfer In = 200 KG

Result:

Warehouse A:
1,000 - 200 = 800 KG

Warehouse B:
500 + 200 = 700 KG

Company total:

1,000 + 500 = 1,500 KG

After transfer:

800 + 700 = 1,500 KG

Therefore:

TOTAL COMPANY STOCK DOES NOT CHANGE.

Only the LOCATION of the stock changes.
