The customer and vendor ledgers are not functioning as the complete accounting ledger.

This is a critical accounting issue.

Current Problem

Example:
Customer: AHMED

The customer ledger only shows the opening balance journal.

However, AHMED has:

• Sales invoices
• Receipt vouchers
• Customer payments
• Journal entries

These transactions do NOT appear in the customer ledger.

This means the ledger is incomplete.

====================================================
Required Behaviour
====================================================

The ledger must be the complete accounting history for that customer.

Every transaction affecting a customer must appear automatically in the customer's ledger in chronological order.

This includes:

✓ Opening Balance
✓ Sales Invoice
✓ Sales Return
✓ Receipt Voucher
✓ Credit Note
✓ Debit Note
✓ Journal Voucher
✓ Manual Journal Entries
✓ Advance Receipt
✓ Advance Adjustment
✓ Invoice Settlement
✓ Write-off
✓ Payment Allocation
✓ Any future accounting transaction affecting the customer

The same applies to Vendor Ledgers.

====================================================
Single Source of Truth
====================================================

The ledger must become the single accounting source.

All reports must derive balances from ledger/journal postings.

Never calculate balances independently.

====================================================
Code Audit
====================================================

Search the entire codebase.

Find every place where customer/vendor balances are updated.

Find every place where ledger entries are created.

Identify transactions that update balances without creating ledger rows.

Fix them.

No transaction should bypass ledger posting.
