2. CALCULATE GROSS PROFIT USING STANDARD ACCOUNTING
====================================================

Gross Profit must NOT be manually entered.

Calculate it using the standard accounting formula:

Gross Profit =
Net Sales
+ Closing Stock
− Opening Stock
− Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Where:

Net Sales
= Sales - Sales Returns

COGS =
Opening Stock
+ Purchases
+ Direct Expenses
− Closing Stock

Direct Expenses include production/manufacturing costs that are mapped as direct expenses.

Do NOT include:

Rent
Salary
Office expenses
Interest
Administrative expenses
Bank charges
Selling expenses

These belong to indirect expenses.

====================================================
3. CALCULATE NET PROFIT
====================================================

Net Profit must also be automatic.

Net Profit =
Gross Profit
+ Other Income
− Indirect Expenses
− Financial Charges
− Depreciation
− Taxes (if applicable)

Use the existing Chart of Accounts classification.
