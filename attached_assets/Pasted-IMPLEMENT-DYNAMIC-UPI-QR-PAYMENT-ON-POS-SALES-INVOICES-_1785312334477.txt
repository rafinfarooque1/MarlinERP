IMPLEMENT DYNAMIC UPI QR PAYMENT ON POS / SALES INVOICES

IMPORTANT:
Do not redesign or replace the existing invoice system.

Extend the existing POS/Sales invoice, payment, customer outstanding,
PDF, WhatsApp sharing, accounting and reconciliation flows.

The key business rule is:

UPI QR AMOUNT = CURRENT OUTSTANDING AMOUNT

NOT the original invoice amount.

==========================================================
1. CORE BUSINESS RULE
==========================================================

Every invoice must calculate:

Invoice Total
- Confirmed/Valid Payments Received
- Valid Credit Adjustments
= Current Outstanding Amount

The UPI QR must always request ONLY the Current Outstanding Amount.

Example:

Invoice Total        ₹5,670
Payment Received     ₹2,000
Outstanding          ₹3,670

The QR must request:

₹3,670

NOT ₹5,670.

If another ₹1,000 is subsequently received:

Invoice Total        ₹5,670
Total Received       ₹3,000
Outstanding          ₹2,670

The next time the invoice is viewed, printed, downloaded or shared,
the QR must automatically request:

₹2,670

No manual QR update should be required.

==========================================================
2. PAYMENT STATUS
==========================================================

Payment status must be derived from actual valid payment/accounting data.

Do not rely only on the original payment mode stored on the sale.

Statuses:

UNPAID

If:

Outstanding Amount = Invoice Total


PARTIALLY PAID

If:

Outstanding Amount > 0
AND
Outstanding Amount < Invoice Total


PAID

If:

Outstanding Amount = 0


CANCELLED

If the invoice has been properly cancelled according to the existing
sales cancellation workflow.

Use the ERP's existing currency precision/rounding rules when comparing
amounts.

==========================================================
3. CREDIT SALES
==========================================================

Credit is a payment arrangement, NOT a permanent payment status.

Example:

Invoice created:

₹10,000
Payment Mode: Credit

Initially:

Invoice Total        ₹10,000
Received                  ₹0
Outstanding          ₹10,000

Status:

UNPAID / CREDIT

QR amount:

₹10,000


Later customer pays:

₹4,000

Invoice becomes:

Invoice Total        ₹10,000
Received              ₹4,000
Outstanding           ₹6,000

Status:

PARTIALLY PAID

QR amount:

₹6,000


Later customer pays remaining:

₹6,000

Invoice becomes:

Invoice Total        ₹10,000
Received             ₹10,000
Outstanding               ₹0

Status:

PAID

QR must disappear.

==========================================================
4. UNPAID INVOICE PAYMENT SECTION
==========================================================

For invoices with an outstanding balance, use the existing:

PAYMENT DETAILS

section.

Display:

PAYMENT DETAILS

SCAN TO PAY (UPI)

[ DYNAMIC QR CODE ]

Invoice Total:       ₹5,670.00
Amount Received:         ₹0.00
Balance Due:         ₹5,670.00

Payment Status: UNPAID

Invoice No: TST/2025-26/0010

The QR must request:

₹5,670.00

==========================================================
5. PARTIALLY PAID INVOICE
==========================================================

For a partially paid invoice display:

PAYMENT DETAILS

SCAN BALANCE TO PAY

[ DYNAMIC QR CODE ]

Invoice Total:       ₹5,670.00
Amount Received:     ₹2,000.00
Balance Due:         ₹3,670.00

Payment Status:

PARTIALLY PAID

The QR must request exactly:

₹3,670.00

==========================================================
6. FULLY PAID INVOICE
==========================================================

If Outstanding Amount = ₹0:

DO NOT SHOW THE UPI QR.

This is critical.

A paid invoice must never display a QR asking the customer to make
another payment.

Instead display:

PAYMENT DETAILS

✓ PAID

Invoice Total:       ₹5,670.00
Amount Received:     ₹5,670.00
Balance Due:             ₹0.00

Payment Status: PAID

Also show available payment information such as:

Payment Method
Payment Date
Reference Number

If multiple payments were used, show an appropriate payment summary.

==========================================================
7. MULTIPLE/PARTIAL PAYMENTS
==========================================================

Support multiple collections against the same invoice.

Example:

Invoice:

₹10,000

Payment 1:
Cash ₹2,000

Payment 2:
Bank ₹3,000

Total Received:

₹5,000

Outstanding:

₹5,000

The QR must request:

₹5,000

Do not assume that one invoice has only one payment.

==========================================================
8. UPI QR GENERATION
==========================================================

Generate the QR dynamically using company payment settings.

QR should contain:

• Company UPI ID
• Payee Name
• Current Outstanding Amount
• Invoice Number / Reference
• Transaction Note

Example concept:

Payee:
Marlin Test

Amount:
₹3,670

Reference:
TST/2025-26/0010

Note:
Payment for Invoice TST/2025-26/0010

Do NOT hard-code UPI information.

==========================================================
9. COMPANY PAYMENT SETTINGS
==========================================================

Use Company Settings as the source for payment information.

Support:

• UPI Enabled
• UPI ID
• UPI Payee Name
• Show UPI QR on Invoice
• Show Bank Details on Invoice
• Default Bank Account

If UPI is disabled or UPI ID is missing:

Do NOT generate an invalid QR.

Show Bank Account Details only.

==========================================================
10. BANK ACCOUNT DETAILS
==========================================================

Continue showing the existing:

BANK ACCOUNT DETAILS

for invoices with an outstanding balance.

Where configured display:

• Account Holder Name
• Bank Name
• Account Number
• IFSC
• Branch

This gives the customer two payment methods:

1. Scan UPI QR
2. Bank Transfer

==========================================================
11. PAYMENT COLLECTION
==========================================================

The QR itself must NOT mark an invoice as paid.

Important:

QR Generated
≠
Payment Received

QR Scanned
≠
Payment Received

Invoice Viewed
≠
Payment Received

PDF Downloaded
≠
Payment Received

WhatsApp Shared
≠
Payment Received

The invoice balance should change only when a valid payment is recorded
through the existing ERP payment/collection/reconciliation process.

==========================================================
12. PAYMENT RECEIVED
==========================================================

When a valid payment is recorded against the invoice:

Immediately recalculate:

Total Received

and

Outstanding Amount.

Example:

Before:

Invoice Total        ₹5,670
Received                  ₹0
Outstanding          ₹5,670

Customer pays:

₹2,000

After:

Invoice Total        ₹5,670
Received              ₹2,000
Outstanding           ₹3,670

The QR should automatically become:

₹3,670

without editing or recreating the invoice.

==========================================================
13. CUSTOMER OUTSTANDING
==========================================================

The invoice outstanding amount must reconcile with the existing customer
receivable/outstanding system.

Do NOT create a second independent outstanding calculation.

Use one authoritative payment/outstanding calculation.

The following should agree:

Invoice Outstanding
Customer Outstanding
Customer Ledger
Accounts Receivable
Sales Outstanding Report

==========================================================
14. ACCOUNTING
==========================================================

Do not create additional accounting entries merely because a QR exists.

QR generation is presentation/payment-request functionality only.

Actual payment accounting must continue through the existing accounting
engine.

Example credit sale:

At Sale:

Debit
Customer / Accounts Receivable

Credit
Sales

Credit
Output GST

according to the existing accounting implementation.


When ₹2,000 is actually received:

Debit
Cash / Bank ₹2,000

Credit
Customer / Accounts Receivable ₹2,000

The remaining receivable stays outstanding.

==========================================================
15. BANK RECONCILIATION
==========================================================

If a bank/UPI payment requires reconciliation under the existing ERP
workflow, continue using that workflow.

Do not mark bank transactions reconciled merely because the invoice
becomes paid unless the existing accounting rules explicitly support it.

Keep separate concepts:

Invoice Payment Status

and

Bank Reconciliation Status.

Example:

Invoice:
PAID

Bank Transaction:
PENDING RECONCILIATION

This must be allowed.

==========================================================
16. PDF MUST ALWAYS USE CURRENT DATA
==========================================================

When generating an invoice PDF, use the latest payment information.

Do not permanently store an old QR amount inside the original invoice.

Example:

Invoice created:

₹5,670 outstanding

Original QR:
₹5,670

Later:

₹2,000 received.

If the user downloads the invoice again:

The newly generated PDF must show:

Received: ₹2,000
Outstanding: ₹3,670
QR: ₹3,670

==========================================================
17. WHATSAPP SHARING
==========================================================

Apply the same rule to WhatsApp invoice sharing.

If Outstanding = ₹3,670:

The shared invoice PDF must show the QR for:

₹3,670

The WhatsApp message may also show:

Invoice Amount: ₹5,670
Amount Received: ₹2,000
Balance Due: ₹3,670

If fully paid:

Do not include payment-request wording.

Instead show that the invoice has been paid.

==========================================================
18. SECURE INVOICE SHARE LINK
==========================================================

The previously implemented secure invoice share link must also generate
the invoice using the CURRENT payment status.

The secure link remains valid according to the existing 15-day expiry
rule.

However, the financial information shown through that link must be
current.

Example:

Day 1:
Outstanding ₹5,670

Day 3:
Customer pays ₹2,000

Same active secure link opened on Day 4 should show:

Received ₹2,000
Outstanding ₹3,670
QR ₹3,670

Do not serve a stale PDF containing the original outstanding amount if
the invoice has subsequently received payment.

==========================================================
19. OVERPAYMENT SAFETY
==========================================================

Prevent accidental overpayment where appropriate.

Example:

Outstanding:

₹3,670

The invoice QR must never request more than:

₹3,670

If a payment entry exceeds the outstanding amount, use the ERP's existing
customer advance/overpayment rules.

Do not silently create negative invoice outstanding.

==========================================================
20. CANCELLED INVOICE
==========================================================

Cancelled invoices must NEVER display an active payment QR.

Display:

CANCELLED

Do not display:

SCAN TO PAY

The secure invoice share/PDF/print views must follow the same rule.

==========================================================
21. SALES RETURN / CREDIT NOTE
==========================================================

If a sales return or credit note reduces the amount due, the outstanding
amount and QR must recalculate accordingly.

Example:

Invoice:

₹10,000

Payment received:

₹4,000

Return/Credit Note:

₹2,000

Adjusted amount due:

₹8,000

Received:

₹4,000

Outstanding:

₹4,000

QR:

₹4,000

Use the existing credit-note/sales-return accounting rules rather than
creating duplicate logic.

==========================================================
FINAL ACCEPTANCE RULE
==========================================================

There must be ONE authoritative Outstanding Amount.

That amount must drive:

1. Invoice Payment Status
2. UPI QR Amount
3. PDF Balance Due
4. WhatsApp Balance Due
5. Secure Share Invoice Balance
6. Customer Outstanding

The fundamental rule is:

UPI QR AMOUNT = CURRENT OUTSTANDING AMOUNT

NEVER:

UPI QR AMOUNT = ORIGINAL INVOICE TOTAL

unless the complete original invoice amount is still outstanding.

Once Outstanding = ₹0:

REMOVE THE QR AND DISPLAY PAID.

After implementation, provide a validation report showing the results of
all tests above and confirm that no duplicate accounting/payment logic
was introduced.
