Enhance the Purchase Bill module by adding a simple "Other Purchase Charges" section.

This is NOT part of inventory cost.

These charges are expenses incurred while purchasing goods and should always be posted directly to the Profit & Loss account.

Examples:
- Freight Expense
- Loading & Unloading
- Hamali
- Courier Charges
- Transportation Expense
- Labour Charges
- Miscellaneous Purchase Expense

========================================================
PURCHASE BILL UI
========================================================

Below the Line Items section, add a new section called:

Other Purchase Charges

Each row should contain:

• Expense Ledger (Dropdown)
• Amount

Buttons:

+ Add Other Charge

Allow unlimited rows.

Example:

Other Purchase Charges

Freight Expense            ₹1,500

Loading Charges            ₹500

Courier Charges            ₹250

+ Add Other Charge

========================================================
EXPENSE LEDGER
========================================================

The dropdown should show only expense ledgers.

Examples:

Freight Expense

Transportation Expense

Loading & Unloading

Packing Expense

Courier Expense

Miscellaneous Purchase Expense

Labour Charges

Other Purchase Expense

Users can create additional expense ledgers from Chart of Accounts, and they should automatically appear in this dropdown.

========================================================
ACCOUNTING ENTRY
========================================================

Purchase of Goods:

Dr Purchase / Inventory

Cr Vendor

Other Purchase Charges:

Dr Selected Expense Ledger

Cr Vendor (or Cash/Bank if paid immediately)

These charges should NEVER be added to inventory valuation.

They should NEVER increase stock cost.

They should NEVER affect weighted average/FIFO valuation.

========================================================
PURCHASE TOTAL
========================================================

Display:

Purchase Value

+ GST

+ Other Purchase Charges

= Grand Total Payable

Show Other Purchase Charges separately in the bill summary.

========================================================
REPORTS
========================================================

The selected expense ledger should appear in:

• Profit & Loss Account
• Ledger Statement
• Day Book
• Trial Balance
• Expense Reports

These charges must NOT appear in Inventory Valuation or Stock Reports.

========================================================
EDIT / DELETE
========================================================

Editing or deleting a Purchase Bill must automatically reverse and recalculate all related expense entries.

========================================================
IMPORT
========================================================

Extend the Purchase Import template to support optional Other Purchase Charges.

Each additional charge should include:

Expense Ledger

Amount

========================================================
BACKWARD COMPATIBILITY
========================================================

Existing Purchase Bills should continue working without changes.

New functionality applies only to newly created or imported Purchase Bills.

The implementation should follow standard ERP accounting practice where incidental purchase expenses are tracked separately in Profit & Loss and not capitalized into inventory.
