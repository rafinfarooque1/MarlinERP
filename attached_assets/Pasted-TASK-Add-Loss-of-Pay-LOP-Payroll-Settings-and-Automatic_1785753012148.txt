TASK: Add Loss of Pay (LOP) Payroll Settings and Automatic Salary Calculation

OBJECTIVE

Enhance the Payroll Settings module by introducing configurable Loss of Pay (LOP) rules. The ERP should automatically calculate payable salary based on configured working days and allowed paid casual leave.

====================================================
1. ADD NEW PAYROLL SETTINGS
====================================================

Under:

Company → Settings → Payroll

Below the existing fields:

• Salary Credit Day
• Full-Day Work Hours
• Half-Day Work Hours

Add the following fields:

1. Working Days Per Month *
   Default: 30

2. Paid Casual Leaves Per Month *
   Default: 4

3. Enable Loss of Pay (Toggle)
   Default: ON

The Payroll Settings section should become:

Salary Credit Day

Full-Day Work Hours

Half-Day Work Hours

Working Days Per Month

Paid Casual Leaves Per Month

Enable Loss of Pay

====================================================
2. LOP CALCULATION LOGIC
====================================================

Example:

Working Days = 30

Paid Casual Leave = 4

Employee Salary = ₹30,000

Employee Attendance:

Present = 26

Leave = 4

Absent = 0

Salary = Full ₹30,000

Because leave is within the paid leave limit.

----------------------------------------------------

Example 2

Working Days = 30

Paid Leave Allowed = 4

Employee Leave = 6

LOP Days = 2

Per Day Salary

30000 / 30 = ₹1000

LOP Deduction

2 × 1000 = ₹2000

Salary Payable

₹28,000

====================================================
3. HALF DAY
====================================================

Half-day should count as:

0.5 Leave

Example

Half Days = 4

Total Leave = 2 Days

This should be included while calculating paid leave.

====================================================
12. ACCOUNTING INTEGRITY
====================================================

LOP deductions must automatically reduce:

• Salary Expense
• Salary Payable

without affecting any other ledgers.

Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Payroll Reports, Employee Ledger and Dashboard must remain fully reconciled.

Expected Result:

The ERP should automatically calculate salary based on configurable Working Days and Paid Casual Leave. Employees who stay within the allowed paid leave receive full salary. Any leave beyond the configured limit is treated as Loss of Pay (LOP), reducing both Salary Expense and Salary Payable automatically while maintaining complete accounting integrity.
