1. ITEM NAME & UNIT FIX (CRITICAL)
====================================================

Currently transfer screens use:

iMap.get(itemId)

This only looks in the Item Master table.

This causes:

• Raw Materials display wrong names
• Packing Materials display wrong names
• Units become incorrect
• "Item #2" appears

Fix:

Build one master lookup using:

item:{id}
raw_material:{id}
packing_material:{id}

Every transfer screen, PDF, Receive screen, Details screen and History screen must use:

materialType + itemId

instead of only itemId.

Never lookup only by integer ID.
