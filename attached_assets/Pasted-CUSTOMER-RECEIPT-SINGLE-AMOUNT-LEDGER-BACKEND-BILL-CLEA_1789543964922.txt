CUSTOMER RECEIPT — SINGLE AMOUNT LEDGER + BACKEND BILL CLEARING
===============================================================

Implement a correction to Customer Receipt accounting and Customer Ledger.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS REQUIREMENT:

When a customer pays a SINGLE amount, the Customer Ledger must show that receipt as ONE SINGLE FINANCIAL TRANSACTION.

Example:

Customer pays ₹10,000.

Customer Ledger MUST show:

Date        Narration          Debit    Credit     Balance
16/09/2026  Receipt Received     —      ₹10,000    ₹XX,XXX

It MUST NOT show the receipt in the ledger as separate bill-wise receipt lines such as:

Invoice A     ₹3,000
Invoice B     ₹4,000
Invoice C     ₹3,000

However, the backend MUST still be able to clear/settle the customer's outstanding invoices using bill allocations.

Therefore:

FINANCIAL RECEIPT
        ↓
ONE ₹10,000 RECEIPT TRANSACTION
        ↓
CUSTOMER LEDGER = ONE ₹10,000 CREDIT
        ↓
BACKEND BILL ALLOCATIONS = ₹10,000 distributed across outstanding bills
        ↓
POS / Customer Outstanding = bills correctly cleared


===============================================================
1. SEPARATE FINANCIAL RECEIPT FROM BILL ALLOCATION
===============================================================

Treat these as two different concepts.

A. FINANCIAL RECEIPT

This is the actual money received from the customer.

Example:

Receipt No: RV-00125
Customer: ABC Customer
Amount: ₹10,000
Payment Mode: Bank
Bank Account: HDFC Bank
Date: 16/09/2026

This creates exactly ONE financial receipt.

The accounting entry must contain:

Bank/Cash        Dr ₹10,000
Customer         Cr ₹10,000

There must be only ONE customer ledger financial entry for this receipt.


B. BILL ALLOCATION

Bill allocation is settlement metadata.

Example:

Receipt ₹10,000

Invoice INV-101 outstanding ₹3,000
Invoice INV-102 outstanding ₹4,000
Invoice INV-103 outstanding ₹3,000

Backend allocation:

INV-101 → ₹3,000
INV-102 → ₹4,000
INV-103 → ₹3,000

These allocations are NOT separate financial receipt transactions.

They must NOT create separate customer ledger receipt rows.

They exist only to determine which invoices are settled.


===============================================================
2. CUSTOMER LEDGER DISPLAY
===============================================================

Customer Ledger must display financial transactions, not bill-allocation rows.

For the above example:

CORRECT:

16/09/2026
Receipt Received
Credit ₹10,000
Balance ₹XX,XXX

ONE ROW ONLY.

Do NOT render:

Receipt INV-101 ₹3,000
Receipt INV-102 ₹4,000
Receipt INV-103 ₹3,000

in the Customer Ledger.

The bill allocation information may be available through:

- receipt details
- expandable receipt row
- View Receipt
- Allocation Details

but must not create additional financial ledger rows.


===============================================================
3. RECEIPT VOUCHER
===============================================================

Receipt Voucher should show:

Customer:
ABC Customer

₹10,000

Payment Mode:
Bank

Bank Account:
HDFC Bank

Payment received

The receipt remains one ₹10,000 transaction.

If bill allocations exist, show them separately under:

INV-102       ₹4,000           ₹4,000        ₹0
INV-103       ₹5,000           ₹3,000        ₹2,000

This is settlement detail only.


===============================================================
4. BACKEND BILL CLEARING
===============================================================

When a single receipt is entered without manually selecting individual bills:

The backend may automatically allocate the receipt against outstanding customer invoices according to the existing settlement policy.

Preferred default:

FIFO by invoice due date / invoice date.

Example:

Receipt = ₹10,000

Outstanding:

INV-101 = ₹3,000
INV-102 = ₹4,000
INV-103 = ₹5,000

Backend allocation:

INV-101 = ₹3,000
INV-102 = ₹4,000
INV-103 = ₹3,000

Remaining:

INV-103 = ₹2,000

BUT:

Customer Ledger still contains:

Receipt Received = ₹10,000

NOT three separate receipt entries.


===============================================================
5. POS OUTSTANDING / BILL STATUS
===============================================================

The POS/customer outstanding system must use the bill allocation table to determine bill settlement.

Therefore:

INV-101 → Cleared
INV-102 → Cleared
INV-103 → Partially Cleared ₹2,000 outstanding

The POS must therefore correctly show the remaining bill balance.

Do NOT change the POS's ability to determine bill-wise outstanding.

The change is only:

LEDGER PRESENTATION
≠
BILL ALLOCATION

Ledger = financial transactions.

POS outstanding = bill settlement state.


===============================================================
6. IF USER EXPLICITLY SELECTS BILL-WISE RECEIPT
===============================================================

The system must support two receipt modes.

MODE 1 — SINGLE AMOUNT RECEIPT

Example:

Customer pays ₹10,000.

User does NOT select bills.

Receipt is recorded as:

₹10,000 single receipt.

Backend may automatically allocate it against outstanding invoices.

Ledger:

ONE ₹10,000 receipt.

MODE 2 — BILL-WISE RECEIPT

User explicitly selects invoices and enters allocations.

Example:

INV-101 → ₹3,000
INV-102 → ₹4,000
INV-103 → ₹3,000

Financial accounting must STILL remain:

ONE ₹10,000 receipt.

Ledger must STILL show:

ONE ₹10,000 receipt.

The bill-wise information is shown only in the receipt allocation/details section.

Do NOT create multiple financial receipt ledger rows even in bill-wise mode.


===============================================================
7. IMPORTANT — DO NOT CREATE DUPLICATE ACCOUNTING
===============================================================

This is critical.

A receipt of ₹10,000 must create:

Bank/Cash Dr ₹10,000
Customer Cr ₹10,000

ONLY ONCE.

Do NOT create:

Customer Cr ₹3,000
Customer Cr ₹4,000
Customer Cr ₹3,000

as separate financial postings.

Bill allocation records must not generate additional customer control-account postings.

The receipt itself is the accounting event.

The allocation only settles invoices.


===============================================================
8. CUSTOMER OUTSTANDING CALCULATION
===============================================================

Customer outstanding must remain mathematically correct.

Use:

Opening Receivable
+ Sales
+ Valid Charges
- Sales Returns / Credit Notes
- Customer Receipts
± Valid Adjustments
= Closing Receivable

A ₹10,000 receipt reduces customer outstanding by exactly ₹10,000.

Bill allocations determine which individual invoices are reduced.

Do not subtract the receipt again through allocations.

Avoid double counting.


===============================================================
9. POS BILL-WISE CLEARING
===============================================================

The user specifically needs the backend bill-wise clearing so that POS can correctly identify cleared invoices.

Therefore maintain:

Receipt Header
    receipt_id
    customer_id
    date
    amount
    payment_mode
    cash/bank_account
    location
    narration

AND separately:

Receipt Allocation
    receipt_id
    invoice_id
    allocated_amount

Example:

Receipt R-100
Amount = ₹10,000

Allocations:

R-100 → INV-101 → ₹3,000
R-100 → INV-102 → ₹4,000
R-100 → INV-103 → ₹3,000

This means:

Financial transaction count = 1
Allocation count = 3

Do not confuse the two.

The backend response itself should have correct financial transaction semantics.

Example:

₹10,000

₹10,000

Not:

₹20,000
-₹3,000
-₹4,000

===============================================================
12. RECEIPT EDIT
===============================================================

When editing a receipt:

Example:

Old receipt:
₹10,000

New receipt:
₹12,000

The system must:

1. Reverse/recalculate the old receipt accounting safely.
2. Recalculate allocations.
3. Create/update the single financial receipt.
4. Ensure customer ledger shows only the final single receipt amount.
5. Ensure POS bill outstanding is correct.
6. Ensure no duplicate accounting postings remain.

Do not leave old allocation postings as financial ledger entries.


===============================================================
13. RECEIPT DELETE / CANCEL
===============================================================

If a receipt is cancelled/deleted according to the existing ERP policy:

- Reverse the single financial receipt.
- Remove/reverse its settlement allocations.
- Reopen affected invoices appropriately.
- Keep audit trail.
- Do not leave orphan bill allocations.
- Do not leave duplicate customer ledger transactions.


===============================================================
14. OVERPAYMENT / CUSTOMER ADVANCE
===============================================================

If receipt amount is greater than total outstanding invoices:

Example:

Outstanding:
₹7,000

₹10,000

Bills = ₹7,000
Customer Advance = ₹3,000

Ledger:

ONE receipt:
₹10,000

Customer advance remains ₹3,000.

Do not create a separate ₹3,000 receipt transaction.

The ₹3,000 is a customer advance/credit balance derived from the same receipt.

Consolidated authorized data.

Do not allow receipt allocations to invoices from unauthorized locations.
