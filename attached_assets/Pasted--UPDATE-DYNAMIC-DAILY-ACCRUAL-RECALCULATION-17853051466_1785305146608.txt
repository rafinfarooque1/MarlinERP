====================================================
UPDATE – DYNAMIC DAILY ACCRUAL & RECALCULATION
====================================================

Update both the Rent Management and Payroll modules to support enterprise-grade dynamic daily accrual accounting.

====================================================
GENERAL PRINCIPLE
====================================================

The ERP must maintain accurate daily Profit & Loss by automatically accruing expenses every day.

However, until the accounting period is Approved, the ERP must allow recalculation if the monthly amount changes.

Approval locks the accounting period.

====================================================
RENT ACCRUAL
====================================================

Every active rent agreement must automatically generate daily accounting entries.

Daily Entry

Debit
Rent Expense (Indirect Expense)

Credit
Rent Payable

Daily Accrual Formula

Monthly Rent
÷
Number of Days in Month

Example

Monthly Rent

₹30,000

July (31 Days)

Daily Accrual

₹967.74

(or according to company rounding policy)

====================================================
RECALCULATION BEFORE APPROVAL
====================================================

If the monthly rent amount changes before the month is Approved,

Example

Original Rent

₹30,000

Changed on July 25

₹60,000

The ERP must automatically

• Recalculate every daily accrual for July.
• Reverse previously generated daily accrual entries.
• Generate corrected daily accrual entries.
• Update

Profit & Loss

Trial Balance

Balance Sheet

General Ledger

Chart of Accounts

Expense Reports

Dashboard

No manual journal entry should be required.

====================================================
AFTER APPROVAL
====================================================

When the month is Approved,

The ERP must

Lock all daily accrual entries.

Prevent automatic recalculation.

Mark the rent as ready for payment.

Approval must NOT generate another Rent Expense or Rent Payable entry because those were already recognized daily.

====================================================
PAYMENT
====================================================

When rent is paid,

Automatically create

Debit

Rent Payable

Credit

Selected Cash or Bank Account

This clears the liability only.

No additional expense should be recorded.

====================================================
PAYROLL
====================================================

Apply exactly the same accounting logic to Payroll.

Every day

Debit

Salary Expense

Credit

Salary Payable

Daily Salary Accrual

Monthly Salary
÷
Number of Days in Payroll Month

====================================================
SALARY RECALCULATION
====================================================

If salary changes before Payroll Approval

Example

Salary

₹30,000

Changed to

₹35,000

before payroll approval.

The ERP must automatically

Reverse previous salary accrual entries.

Generate corrected daily accruals.

Update

Profit & Loss

Trial Balance

Balance Sheet

General Ledger

Chart of Accounts

Payroll Reports

Dashboard

No manual journal entry.

====================================================
PAYROLL APPROVAL
====================================================

Payroll Approval

Locks the payroll period.

Prevents further recalculation.

Does NOT generate duplicate Salary Expense or Salary Payable entries.

====================================================
SALARY PAYMENT
====================================================

When salary is paid,

Automatically create

Debit

Salary Payable

Credit

Cash or Bank

No additional expense posting.

====================================================
ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLE
====================================================

At the close of every business day, the ERP must provide an accurate financial position.

The following should already be reflected:

• Sales
• Purchases
• Production Cost
• Rent Expense
• Salary Expense
• Other Expenses
• Cash
• Bank
• GST
• Receivables
• Payables

Once all daily transactions are entered and bank reconciliation is completed, the ERP should automatically produce accurate:

• Chart of Accounts
• Trial Balance
• Profit & Loss
• Balance Sheet
• Cash Flow
• General Ledger

without requiring any manual journal entries.

====================================================
AUDIT
====================================================

Whenever recalculation occurs,

Record

• Previous Amount
• New Amount
• Previous Daily Accrual
• New Daily Accrual
• User
• Date
• Time
• Reason
• Number of entries reversed
• Number of entries regenerated

Maintain a complete audit trail.

====================================================
FINAL REQUIREMENT
====================================================

The ERP must follow a true accrual accounting model.

Daily expenses should always reflect the latest unapproved values.

Once a period is Approved, it becomes financially locked. Any subsequent changes must follow the ERP's controlled adjustment or period reopening process with appropriate permissions and a full audit trail.
