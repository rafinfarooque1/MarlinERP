PART 1 — DIAGNOSE THE ROOT CAUSE
====================================================

First inspect an existing affected Asset Purchase in the LIVE database.

Trace the complete flow:

Asset Purchase
→ asset_purchases record
→ payment/vendor selection
→ accounting voucher/journal
→ journal header
→ journal lines
→ ledger/account postings
→ Vendor Ledger
→ Fixed Asset Ledger
→ Trial Balance
→ Chart of Accounts
→ location filtering

For every existing affected Asset Purchase, determine:

- Was the Asset Purchase record created correctly?
- Was a voucher/journal created?
- Was the voucher posted?
- Is the voucher balanced?
- Are the correct debit and credit journal lines present?
- Which ledger/account IDs were used?
- Is the Fixed Asset account line present?
- Is the Vendor/Creditor account line present for credit purchases?
- Are the debit/credit directions correct?
- Is the journal/voucher location_type present?
- Is the journal/voucher location_id present?
- Are the accounts available for that location?
- Are the journal lines being excluded by the Vendor Ledger query?
- Are the journal lines being excluded by the Fixed Asset Ledger query?
- Are existing vouchers missing location data?
- Are ledger/account mappings missing because of the new multi-location accounting changes?

Compare an affected Asset Purchase against a normal Purchase/Voucher that correctly appears in the relevant ledgers.

Find the exact root cause before implementing the repair.

====================================================
PART 2 — REQUIRED ACCOUNTING POSTINGS
====================================================

The existing ERP accounting architecture and conventions must be preserved.

For a Vendor/Credit Asset Purchase, the accounting result must effectively be:

Fixed Asset Account / Asset Ledger       DEBIT
Vendor / Creditor Ledger                 CREDIT

For example:

Asset Purchase: ₹100,000 on credit from Vendor A

Fixed Asset Account     Dr ₹100,000
Vendor A                Cr ₹100,000

Result:

- The Asset Purchase must appear in the Fixed Asset Ledger.
- The Asset Purchase must appear in Vendor A's Ledger.
- Vendor outstanding/payable must update correctly.
- Trial Balance must remain balanced.

For Cash Asset Purchases:

Fixed Asset Account     DEBIT
Cash Account            CREDIT

The transaction must appear in:

- Fixed Asset Ledger
- Cash Ledger

For Bank Asset Purchases:

Fixed Asset Account     DEBIT
Bank Account            CREDIT

The transaction must appear in:

- Fixed Asset Ledger
- Bank Ledger

For any other existing payment modes, follow the ERP's existing accounting rules.

Do not invent a new accounting model if the current ERP already has established posting rules.

PART 5 — FIX EXISTING LIVE/PRODUCTION TRANSACTIONS
====================================================

There are already Asset Purchases entered in LIVE that are missing from the Vendor Ledger and/or Fixed Asset Ledger.

These must be repaired safely.

Create a production-safe, IDEMPOTENT repair/backfill process.

The repair process must:

1. Scan existing Asset Purchases.

2. Identify whether each Asset Purchase already has:

   - a valid accounting voucher/journal
   - a Fixed Asset ledger posting
   - a Vendor ledger posting where required
   - correct debit/credit lines
   - correct location_type
   - correct location_id

3. For transactions that are already correct:
   - DO NOTHING.
   - Do not duplicate anything.

4. For transactions that are missing required accounting postings:
   - Repair only the missing or invalid posting.
   - Reuse the existing Asset Purchase data.
   - Preserve the original purchase date.
   - Preserve the original Vendor.
   - Preserve the original Asset.
   - Preserve the original amount.
   - Preserve the original transaction/reference where possible.
   - Use the original selected location.

5. Prevent duplicates.

The repair MUST be idempotent.

Running it:

Once → fixes missing entries.

Running it again → must not create duplicate vouchers or duplicate ledger lines.

Before inserting anything, check for:

- existing repair voucher/reference
- existing linked voucher
- existing journal lines
- existing account posting
- existing Vendor posting
- existing Fixed Asset posting

Do not create a second accounting transaction if a valid transaction already exists.
