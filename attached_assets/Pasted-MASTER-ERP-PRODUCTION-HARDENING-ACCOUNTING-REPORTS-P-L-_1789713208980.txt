4. PROFIT & LOSS — CRITICAL
============================================================

Audit and correct P&L completely.

P&L must explicitly follow a deterministic accounting definition.

At minimum:

NET SALES
    = Gross Sales
      - Sales Returns
      - Sales Discounts/Credit Notes
      ± valid sales adjustments

COGS
    = Opening Inventory
      + Net Purchases/Inventory Cost Additions
      + applicable direct inventory costs
      - Closing Inventory

GROSS PROFIT
    = Net Sales - COGS

NET PROFIT
    = Gross Profit
      + Other/Direct Income where applicable
      - Indirect Expenses
      - Depreciation
      ± other valid P&L items

IMPORTANT:

Do not double count:

- production consumption
- production output
- purchase cost
- stock transfers
- inventory adjustments
- direct expenses
- indirect expenses
- depreciation
- sales returns
- purchase returns

Production must not create artificial profit.

Internal warehouse transfers must not create revenue.

Internal stock transfers must not create COGS.

Inventory movements must not themselves create profit.

Purchase of inventory does not become expense immediately if the accounting model uses inventory capitalization.

Only consumed/sold inventory becomes COGS according to the ERP's inventory accounting model.
