==================================================
PART A — WHY IS A SALES INVOICE SHOWING AS RECEIPT?
==================================================

Current example visible in Accounts → Vouchers:

Type: Receipt
Voucher #: INV/2025-26/0001
Description: Ragiguda Sales → Fasin
Amount: ₹5,320

Investigate exactly why a SALES INVOICE is appearing as a RECEIPT voucher.

These are different accounting events.

A SALES INVOICE represents revenue / customer receivable.

A RECEIPT represents money actually received.

They must not be treated as the same event unless the sale was genuinely paid immediately and the ERP intentionally generated a corresponding receipt transaction.

==================================================
1. CORRECT ACCOUNTING MODEL
==================================================

For CREDIT SALE:

Invoice posting should normally be:

Dr Customer / Sundry Debtors
Cr Sales
Cr Output GST, where applicable

This creates customer receivable.

It must NOT create a Receipt Voucher merely because the invoice was saved.

Later, when customer pays:

Dr Cash / Bank / appropriate payment-clearing ledger
Cr Customer

THAT payment should be represented as a Receipt Voucher.

--------------------------------------------------

For IMMEDIATE CASH/BANK SALE:

If POS records payment at the time of sale, the ERP may create the appropriate receipt/payment settlement automatically.

For example:

Sales accounting:
Dr Customer / settlement mechanism
Cr Sales
Cr Output GST

Receipt:
Dr Cash / Bank / payment clearing
Cr Customer

OR another valid equivalent accounting design already used by this ERP.

But the system must preserve the distinction:

INVOICE = sale
RECEIPT = collection of money

Do not simply label invoices as Receipt Vouchers.

10. CONTRA EDITING
==================================================

Contra represents movement between cash/bank accounts.

Cr Cash

12. IMPORTANT — ALLOCATION VS LEDGER BALANCE
==================================================

Do not blindly assume:

ledger balance = invoice outstanding

There can be:

- partial payments

- credit notes
- debit notes
- journals
- opening balances

Preserve invoice-level allocation where required.

But at account/customer/vendor summary level, the ERP must reconcile logically with the General Ledger.

If the system has both:

and
GL balance

explicitly define and reconcile them.
